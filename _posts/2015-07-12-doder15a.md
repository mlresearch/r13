abstract: The main goal of this work is to present the proof-theoretical and model-theoretical
  approach to a probabilistic logic which allows reasoning about temporal information.
  We extend both the language of linear time logic and the language of probabilistic
  logic, allowing statements like “A will always hold"and “the probability that A
  will hold in next moment is at least the probability that B will always hold," where
  A and B are arbitrary statements. We axiomatize this logic, provide corresponding
  semantics and prove that the axiomatization is sound and strongly complete. We show
  that the problem of deciding decidability is PSPACE-complete, no worse than that
  of linear time logic.
