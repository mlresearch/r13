abstract: We consider the problem of learning perturbation-based probabilistic models
  by computing and differentiating expected losses. This is a challenging computational
  problem that has traditionally been tackled using Monte Carlo-based methods. In
  this work, we show how a generalization of parametric min-cuts can be used to address
  the same problem, achieving high accuracy of faster than a sampling-based baseline.
  Utilizing our proposed \textit{Skeleton Method}, we show that we can learn the perturbation
  model so as to directly minimize expected losses. Experimental results show that
  this approach offers promise as a new way of training structured prediction models
  under complex loss functions.
