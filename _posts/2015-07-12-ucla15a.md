abstract: Learning graphical model parameters from incomplete data is a non-convex
  optimization problem. Iterative algorithms, such as Expectation Maximization (EM),
  can be used to get a local optimum solution. However, little is known about the
  quality of the learned local optimum, compared to the unknown global optimum. We
  exploit variables that are always observed in the dataset to get an upper bound
  on the global optimum which can give insight into the quality of the parameters
  learned by estimation algorithms.
