abstract: Dependency graphs (also called association graphs or bidirected graphs)
  represent marginal independencies amongst a set of variables. We give a characterization
  of the directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) that faithfully explain a given dependency
  graph in terms of their transitive closures, and use it to efficiently enumerate
  such structures. Our results map out the space of faithful causal models for given
  marginal independence relations, and show to which extent causal inference is possible
  without using conditional independence tests.
