abstract: For undirected graphical models, belief propagation often performs remarkably
  well for approximate marginal inference, and may be viewed as a heuristic to minimize
  the Bethe free energy. Focusing on binary pairwise models, we demonstrate that several
  recent results on the Bethe approximation may be generalized to a broad family of
  related pairwise free energy approximations with arbitrary counting numbers. We
  explore comparisons to the true (Gibbs) free energy and shed light on the empirical
  success of the Bethe approximation.
